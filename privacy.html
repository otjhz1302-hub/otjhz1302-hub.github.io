Privacy Policy

Somnia · Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: Somnia never asks for your name, email, location, or access to your microphone, contacts or photos. Your mixes and settings are stored on your phone. We use an anonymous identifier to count likes and plays, and Google AdMob shows ads. Searches are only shared if you turn that on. You can delete your data from Settings → Your data.

1. Who is responsible

This policy applies to the Somnia app for Android and its web version (“Somnia”, “we”). The data controller is OTJH LLC, United States. You can write to us at otjhllc@gmail.com with any privacy question.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Somnia is free and has no in-app purchases. It shows native ads labeled “Ad” and, only if you choose to, rewarded videos to create more (an extra layer, a mix slot or the Full Night). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID, cookies or similar technologies to show ads, cap their frequency, measure results and detect fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, your choice is requested through Google's consent platform (UMP). If you decline, non-personalized ads or no ads are shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Who we share data with

  • Supabase (database and anonymous authentication), as a processor.
  • Google (AdMob), as an independent controller for advertising.
  • Vercel (hosting for the web version).
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where applicable, they rely on recognized safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Likes and favorite songs linked to the anonymous id: until you delete them.
  • Individual play records: 7 days. After that only the song's total remains, linked to no one.
  • Shared searches: 30 days. After that only aggregate counts remain, linked to no device.
  • Published mixes: while they are published. If you delete your data, they remain in the library anonymously, with no link to you.
  • Advertising data: according to Google's policy.

8. How to delete your data

In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete my data. This removes your likes, favorites, plays and shared searches from our server and creates a new device id. Each song's public totals are kept without identifying anyone.

Without the app: email otjhllc@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Somnia data”. We reply within 30 days.

Uninstalling the app erases everything that was stored only on your phone.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR in Europe, CCPA/CPRA in California, LGPD in Brazil), you may request access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing. Write to the address in section 1. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

Somnia is not directed to children under 13 (16 in some EU countries) and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Communications are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), the device id is stored on our server only as a hash, and database access is restricted by per-user rules. No system is perfect, but we apply reasonable measures to protect information.

12. Health

Somnia offers ambient sounds for your personal rest, focus or relaxation routine. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not collect health data.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is material, let you know in the app.
